The contact form is intended for conversations about my work and software engineering opportunities.

What the form collects

Your name, email address, optional company name and message, together with a receipt timestamp and a request identifier. Please do not include passwords, financial details or other sensitive information.

How messages are handled

When the form is enabled, messages are saved in a private Google Sheet and an email notification is sent to me so I can respond. The spreadsheet is not published or accessible from this website. Google processes the information as part of these services.

Spam protection

The live form uses Cloudflare Turnstile. Its verification service processes technical browser and connection information to protect the form against abuse. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Questions or deletion requests

Email n0t_l3ss@outlook.com if you have a question about a message you sent or would like it deleted.
